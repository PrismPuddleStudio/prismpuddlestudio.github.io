Privacy Policy for Number Garden - Color By Number

Developer: Prism Puddle Studios

Contact: PrismPuddleStudios@protonmail.com

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Number Garden - Color By Number ("the app") is made by Prism Puddle Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. The app works without signing up.

Saved on your device only. Your coloring progress, hint balance, completed pictures, and settings are stored locally on your phone. We do not receive or store this information on our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases. If you buy hint packs or No Ads, the payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We only receive confirmation that a purchase happened so we can give you what you bought.

Advertising

The app shows ads, including video ads and banner ads, through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Your IP address and approximate location (for example, your country)
  • Device type, operating system, and app usage related to ads

On iPhone, we ask for permission before your advertising ID is used for personalized ads. You can say no and still use the app. You will just see less personalized ads. On Android, you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you buy No Ads, the app stops showing ads.

Children

The app is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only outside services the app uses are Google AdMob for ads, and Apple or Google for purchases, as described above.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in your phone's settings.
  • Buy No Ads to remove ads.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally saved data.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
