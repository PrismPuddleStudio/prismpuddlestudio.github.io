Privacy Policy for Prism Puddle Studios

Developer: Prism Puddle Studios

Contact: PrismPuddleStudios@protonmail.com

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy applies to our mobile games and apps, including Number Garden - Color By Number, Tile Garden, and Hive & Nest (together, "the apps"). They are made by Prism Puddle Studios ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the apps use and why.

Information we collect (all apps)

We do not ask for your name, email, or any account. The apps work without signing up.

Saved on your device only. Progress, settings, and other play data are stored locally on your phone. We do not receive or store this information on our servers. Deleting an app deletes that app's saved data on your device.

We do not sell your personal information.

Number Garden - Color By Number

Number Garden saves your coloring progress, hint balance, completed pictures, and settings on your device.

Purchases and ads are not turned on in the current version. A future update may add optional hint packs, a one-time No Ads purchase, and ads (including video ads) through Google AdMob. If that happens, we will update this policy and the in-app privacy link before those features go live. Payment would be handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We would never see your card or payment details. We would only receive confirmation that a purchase happened so we can give you what you bought.

Until ads are turned on, Number Garden does not use Google AdMob and does not collect an advertising ID for ads.

Tile Garden

Tile Garden saves your level progress, Daily Garden streak, My Garden progress, leftover helpers, and sound settings on your device.

Right now Tile Garden has no ads and no real-money purchases. A later update may offer an optional rewarded ad when helpers run out. If that happens, we will update this policy first.

Hive & Nest

Hive & Nest saves your level progress, coin balance, power-ups, upgrades, and sound settings on your device.

Coins, power-ups, and upgrades are earned and spent inside the game only. There are no real-money purchases and no ads in the current version. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Advertising (only when an app actually shows ads)

When an app shows ads through Google AdMob, Google may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iPhone, Advertising ID on Android)
  • Your IP address and approximate location (for example, your country)
  • Device type, operating system, and app usage related to ads

On iPhone, we ask for permission before your advertising ID is used for personalized ads. You can say no and still use the app. You will just see less personalized ads. On Android, you can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

Learn how Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If an app offers No Ads and you buy it, that app stops showing ads.

This section does not apply until ads are turned on in that app.

Children

The apps are designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. When ads or store purchases are turned on in an app, the only outside services that app uses for those features are Google AdMob for ads, and Apple or Google for purchases, as described above.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalized ads in your phone's settings (when an app shows ads).
  • Buy No Ads, if that app offers it, to remove ads.
  • Delete an app to remove all of that app's locally saved data.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
